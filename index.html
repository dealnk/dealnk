the story

about.

deal nk started out rapping in spanish—then tore it all down. almost every old release is gone, replaced by a full reinvention: pop-punk and alt-rock, written and sung entirely in english, aimed at an audience an ocean away from home.

the songs live in the same place as the reference points: self-destructive cycles, burnout, defiance toward anyone counting you out—delivered with imagery over statement, and a hook built to survive a room full of strangers screaming it back.
